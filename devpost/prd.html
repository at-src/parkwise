ParkWise

A browser-based, interactive 3D parking simulator where a learner drives a physics-based car to practice real parking maneuvers — the coach demonstrates first, then guides her own attempt.

Features & Behavior (tap to expand acceptance criteria)

Driving the car — interactive, physics-based ›
  • Arrow keys / WASD control forward, reverse, and steering.
  • Turns based on steering + motion (no strafing/teleporting); reverses believably.
  • Responds immediately and continuously while a key is held.
The coach — demonstrate, then guide ›
  • On landing, the coach auto-demonstrates: the car drives itself through a correct park.
  • A "Practice" action hands control to the learner.
  • During her attempt, guidance appears near the car: distance ahead/behind + how much to adjust.
  • Guidance updates live as the car moves.
Camera views ›
  • Four switchable views: top-down, in-car, chase, free isometric 3D.
  • Switching views doesn't interrupt driving or reset the car.
Parking check — pass / fail ›
  • "Parked correctly" = within the markings, roughly straight, not touching parked cars or curb.
  • Correct park → clear success verdict.
  • Not correct → no success; she keeps adjusting.
Reset & scenario switching ›
  • Reset returns the car to the scenario start.
  • Switcher moves across the core scenarios — parallel, reverse bay, angle — with room for more if time permits.
Sensor sound if it fits ›
  • Beeps that speed up as the car nears an obstacle (likely Web Audio API).
  • Included if it fits the PoC; approach decided at build.

States

  • Arrival — lands in a scenario; coach demo plays automatically.
  • Demo playing — car drives itself; her input not required until Practice.
  • Practicing — she has control; guidance is live.
  • Success — correct park shows the verdict.
  • Reset — car returns to the start.
  • Persistence — nothing stored; reopening starts fresh.

Scope Guardrails

Building now ›
  • Single-screen interactive 3D scene (Three.js), loads straight into a scenario.
  • Physics-based, keyboard-driven car.
  • Four switchable camera views.
  • Coach: auto-demo, then live guidance near the car.
  • Pass/fail parking check; reset; switch across the core scenarios (parallel, reverse bay, angle).
  • Proximity sensor sound (if it fits).
Deferred from the PoC ›
  • Saved progress / accounts — nothing persists.
  • Scenarios beyond the core set — optional additions if time permits, not committed.
  • Deployment to a public URL (optional; submission needs repo + demo video).
  • Mouse steering — keyboard first.
Non-goals ›
  • Open world / free driving — the point is the maneuvers.
  • AI or moving traffic — not needed to prove the kernel; static obstacles suffice.
  • Partial / arcade scoring — pass/fail only.

Open Questions (none block the spec)

  • Exact on-screen form of coach guidance (numbers vs. arrows) — decide once the scene renders.
  • Mouse controls alongside keyboard — build-time call.
  • Whether sensor sound makes the PoC cut — add if it fits.
