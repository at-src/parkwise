ParkWise app map

Three files do all the work: index.html (the page and every control), style.css (the paper-style HUD) and app.js (the 3D yard, the physics, the coach). app.js is one file split into sections, each starting with a // ===== banner. Search for a banner to jump straight to it.

The one idea that holds it together

The model decides, everything else draws. A car is a pose: the back axle's x, z, a heading th and the wheel st (−1…1). stepPose() moves it like a real car (a kinematic bicycle: it turns about one point on the rear-axle line, R = wheelbase / tan(steer)). The meshes, labels, wall boards and HUD only read that pose. That's why the whole look could change without touching the physics.

One frame, start to finish

tick()→stepClock / driveStep→CUR pose→applyCar (meshes)→explanation layer (lines + tags)→redrawHolo (wall boards)→syncDom (HUD)→render

Watching: the clock TT runs along a pre-integrated path and poseAt gives the pose. Practising: driveStep() moves the car from your keys in 2 cm pieces, and touchAt() stops it at a car, a kerb or the slab's edge.

Where to change what

  1. Colours of the yard: WALL, BAND, TRIM in the showroom section; floor tiles in FLOOR.
  2. Car paints: PAINTS in the sets section (sage is the learner car).
  3. Daylight and shine: VIEW in the light section (refl, bloomS, exp).
  4. Words: step titles in MAN, explanations in STEP_WORDS, coach prompts in COACH, help in index.html's dialog.
  5. HUD look: CSS variables at the top of style.css.

Practice: reuse it yourself

Change the slope from 1 in 10 to 1 in 6. Find HILL in the sets section and set grade to 1 / 6. Reload and play the Slope manoeuvre. The slab tilts more, the roll-back distance in the text changes, and the "kN" figure goes up, all without editing any text, because the words read FACT and HILL. Then put it back.
